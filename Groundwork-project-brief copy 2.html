The Brief

A project has just kicked off. You're the designer tasked with wowing the client on the very first presentation. Using a fictitious product brief (provided below), you'll run through the entire design process with Claude as your co-designer — generating concepts, iterating on them, and shipping a prototype that's not just functional, but surprising.

Stretch Goal

Create a small style guide / design system alongside the prototype, using Claude as your design systems partner.

Tips for this project
  • Use Impress Generate for concept generation and iteration
  • Run Impress Evaluate or Impeccable to score and critique your work
  • Use Impeccable for senior-level design critique
  • Use Claude for production-level work
  • Connect to Figma + Figma MCP for creative exploration

How to use Claude effectively

Claude is your co-designer, not a search engine. The designers who get the most out of it treat it like a senior collaborator — give it context, push back on its output, and iterate in conversation rather than starting from scratch each time.

01

Start broad — ask Claude to generate 3 stylistically distinct concepts before converging on a direction.

02

Use explicit critique prompts: "What's weak about this design? What would a senior designer change?"

03

Describe the user, not just the screen — give Claude context about who's using this and why.

04

Iterate in conversation — each screen should build on the last, not restart fresh each time.

05

For novel features, ask: "What's a feature here that would surprise and delight a user?"

06

Give Claude feedback and explicitly tell it to document (remember) that feedback for future sessions.

Project Brief
Groundwork
Small business lending

Groundwork is a fintech startup offering working capital loans to small businesses — think the local catering company that needs to buy equipment before a big contract, or the independent retailer bridging a slow season. They are not a bank. They don't want to feel like one. Their promise: fast, fair, human — money that works with you, not against you.

Design Challenge

Design the end-to-end digital experience for a small business owner applying for and managing a working capital loan. The experience spans three core moments.

Owner Dashboard
  • Loan summary + status
  • Repayment schedule
  • Quick actions
  • Business health snapshot
Loan Application
  • Short, low-anxiety flow
  • Amount + purpose
  • Basic business info
  • Soft eligibility check
  • Status + follow-up
Application Tracker
  • Decision + offer review
  • Document requests
  • Ongoing loan management
User Scenarios
Rosa
Catering company · 6 employees

Needs $30k to buy a commercial oven before a contracted event season. Not financially savvy. Distrusts banks after a bad experience.

"Needs to feel respected, not judged."

Marcus
Independent hardware store

Has used traditional SBA loans before. Wants speed and transparency — he's done waiting 6 weeks for a decision.

"Values plain language over financial jargon."

Priya
Co-founder, streetwear brand

Tech-native. Expects a clean, app-quality experience. Skeptical of anything that feels corporate or generic.

"Wants to see her numbers, not be talked at."

Brand Direction
This is Groundwork
  • Warm, direct, confident
  • Plain English — always
  • Feels like a trusted advisor
  • Progress over complexity
  • Celebrates small business wins
  • Calm in moments of stress
This is not Groundwork
  • No bank-blue palettes
  • No dense legal language upfront
  • Not cheeky, startup-quirky
  • No hollow motivational copy
  • Not sterile or clinical
  • No jargon without explanation
