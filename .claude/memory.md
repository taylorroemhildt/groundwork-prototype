## Project Overview
Groundwork is a fintech startup offering working capital loans to small businesses. The product is a web app covering three core flows: Owner Dashboard, Loan Application, and Application Tracker. Brand promise is fast, fair, human — warm and direct, not bank-like or corporate.

## Research Highlights
- **Show offer early:** Soft eligibility check with visible estimated terms before commitment is the highest-impact trust/conversion mechanism in category
- **Plain dollars beat APR:** "You'll pay back $40,250" resonates far better than rate jargon for non-financially-savvy owners
- **Dashboard as relationship:** Milestone acknowledgment, plain-language status, and business health context transform a transactional product into a trusted advisor
- **Human availability = trust signal:** Even if rarely used, a human escalation option at high-anxiety moments (offer review, problems) dramatically increases digital trust
- **Every negative state needs explanation + next step:** Unexplained freezes/declines are the defining failure pattern across all major competitors
