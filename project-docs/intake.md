# Intake

**Client:** Groundwork
**Product Type:** Web app
**Product Description:** End-to-end digital experience for small business working capital loans. Three core areas: (1) Owner Dashboard — loan summary, repayment schedule, quick actions, business health snapshot; (2) Loan Application — short low-anxiety flow, amount + purpose, basic business info, soft eligibility check, status + follow-up; (3) Application Tracker — decision + offer review, document requests, ongoing loan management.
**Industry:** Fintech / Small business lending
**Primary Personas / Use Cases:**
- Rosa (catering company, 6 employees) — needs $30k for equipment, not financially savvy, distrusts banks. "Needs to feel respected, not judged."
- Marcus (independent hardware store) — experienced with SBA loans, wants speed and transparency. "Values plain language over financial jargon."
- Priya (co-founder, streetwear brand) — tech-native, expects app-quality UX, skeptical of anything corporate or generic. "Wants to see her numbers, not be talked at."

**UI Look and Feel:** Warm, direct, confident. Plain English always. Feels like a trusted advisor, not a bank. Progress over complexity. Calm in moments of stress. No bank-blue palettes, no dense legal language upfront, no hollow motivational copy, not sterile or clinical.
**Design System:** shadcn/ui
