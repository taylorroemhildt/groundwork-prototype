## Design Context

### Users
Small business owners applying for and managing working capital loans. Three personas: Rosa (catering, not financially savvy, distrusts banks), Marcus (hardware store, experienced, wants transparency and speed), Priya (streetwear brand co-founder, tech-native, expects app-quality UX). Used primarily during business hours on desktop, occasionally mobile. High-stakes moments: loan application, offer review, repayment management.

### Brand Personality
Warm, direct, confident. "Trusted advisor" — not a bank, not a startup. Plain English always. Calm in moments of stress. Celebrates small business wins. Anti-references: bank-blue corporate palettes, dense legal language, hollow motivational copy, sterile/clinical UIs, cheeky startup-quirky tone.

### Aesthetic Direction
Editorial fintech — the visual warmth of a trusted local business advisor combined with the precision of a well-designed financial product. Forest green (#1A5C3D) as the single accent — earthy, grounded, trustworthy. White cards on #FAFAFA background. Light mode only (used in business-hours context, high-stakes financial decisions require clarity and calm). Typography should feel authoritative but approachable — not a bank, not a VC-backed startup.

### Design Principles
1. **Calm confidence** — every screen should reduce anxiety, not add to it. Generous whitespace, clear hierarchy, no visual noise.
2. **Plain over clever** — labels, copy, and numbers should be immediately comprehensible by a non-financially-savvy owner.
3. **Human warmth** — personalization (first name greetings, milestone moments), warm tone, always a human available.
4. **Progress over complexity** — show forward momentum, not financial complexity. Progress bars, milestone celebrations, "you're on track" over raw data.
5. **Single accent, used intentionally** — green means important, actionable, or positive. Not decorative.
